# 图标生成提示词

模式：内置 imagegen；类别：logo-brand。2026-10-08。

原始输出保存为本目录 source.png，1254×1254、RGB、无 alpha；工程通过脚本等比规范为 1024×1024。仅做尺寸和色彩空间打包，不重绘图案。内置生成不是确定性绘图，复刻发布文件应使用保存的 source.png。

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production iOS app icon for a personal bookkeeping app.
Primary request: a beautiful, minimal, immediately recognizable and memorable icon that clearly conveys recording personal spending and money.
Subject: one cohesive emblem combining a small upright closed accounting ledger with a prominent gold coin. The ledger has a quiet slim binding spine and just two short entry marks. A large coin is integrated into the lower-right of the ledger, with a very clear simple ¥ currency mark. Coin and ledger must read as one confident symbol, not a collage of unrelated objects.
Style: exceptionally clean flat vector-like graphic, bold geometric silhouette, soft carefully balanced corners, crisp edges, refined optical alignment. Friendly but mature utility app identity. Three solid colors: deep forest green for the ledger and currency mark, warm rich gold for the coin, warm ivory full-bleed background. Keep the accounting page details ivory. High contrast. No gradients, textures, bevels or shadows.
Composition: a single centered logo, approximately 65–70% of the square width, generous equal quiet margins, balanced optical weight. Money coin large enough to remain recognizable at 40–60 pixels. Ledger is unmistakably a book, not a wallet, phone or document stack. Coin has one flat clean face, no tiny rim ticks or ornate borders.
Text: only the ¥ mark on the coin; no app name, words, letters, numbers or slogans.
Output: one finished square 1024x1024 opaque app icon, artwork only. Background extends to all four edges. Do not pre-round or mask the outer square; the operating system will apply its icon shape.
Avoid: emoji rendering, cartoon face, piggy bank, multiple loose coins, charts, floating decorations, sparkles, notebook spiral holes, thin detail, mockup framing, app screens, watermarks.
```
