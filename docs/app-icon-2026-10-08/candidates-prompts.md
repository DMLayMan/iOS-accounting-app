# 两个新方向的生成提示词

内置 imagegen，logo-brand，2026-10-08。两个候选用于对比，不代表用户已经选定。

## 账袋 · candidate-bag.png

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset: an original iOS personal bookkeeping app icon, square, full-bleed and opaque.
Create ONE distinctive, simple and elegant emblem: a rounded money pouch whose small folded opening subtly resembles the page edge of a ledger. The money pouch itself IS the accounting symbol; do not add a separate notebook or coin. The body has a big clean ¥ mark cut out in deep green. A tiny warm-ivory folded ledger tab at the neck makes the money-and-recordkeeping idea memorable, but the overall silhouette remains instantly recognizable as a money pouch.
Style: impeccably clean flat graphic with rich solid fills, bold smooth contours and precise optical balance. Warm, mature, friendly, not cartoonish. No line clutter and no rendering noise.
Colors: a deep pine-green full-bleed square background; one warm-gold pouch silhouette; ivory confined to the small page fold. Three colors only. Yuan mark uses the same pine-green as the background.
Composition: exactly one emblem centered, about 62% canvas width, generous safe margins, readable at 40 and 60 px. The pouch has a broad rounded base and a gently pinched neck, one simple folded top, no string bow or tiny knots. No ledger row marks in the body; keep the ¥ the only internal symbol.
Output one finished square 1024x1024 icon artwork, not a mockup, not an options grid. Keep all four outer corners square and fill the entire background. No app title or letters, no shadows, no bevels, no gradients, no textures, no loose coins, no pig, no faces, no sparkles, no brand references or watermarks. Only permitted glyph is ¥.
```

## 折页账单 · candidate-sheet.png

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset: a finished iOS personal accounting app icon.
Design a refined, extremely simple icon around ONE folded account sheet. One upright warm-ivory sheet has softly rounded corners and a single folded upper-right corner. The folded corner is deep pine green. The face shows one large beautifully balanced pine-green ¥ mark and two short broad ledger-entry bars beneath it. It should convey recording money in one cohesive silhouette, without stacking multiple objects.
A rich warm-gold solid background fills the entire square. The combination of an ivory account sheet, a compact deep-green fold, and the bold yuan mark should feel calm, memorable and confident. Rich flat colors, crisp vector-like edges, precise spacing, subtle friendly corner geometry. No physical paper rendering.
Composition: large centered single silhouette occupying around 60% of canvas width and 70% height, visually balanced, clear at 40–60 px. Two entry bars are short, separated and subordinate to the currency mark. Only three solid colors: warm gold, warm ivory, deep pine green.
One square 1024x1024 opaque artwork. Full bleed background and square outer corners; the OS applies its icon mask. No app name or text other than ¥. No money bag, no separate coin, no chart, no numbers, no detailed borders, no gradient, no shadow, no glossy 3D, no texture, no floating marks, no watermark, no frame, no phone or home-screen mockup.
```
