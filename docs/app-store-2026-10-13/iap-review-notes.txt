This consumable is an optional, one-time tip to support the developer. It does not unlock features, grant virtual currency or other benefits, or start a subscription.

Path: open the app, select the Me tab (我的), then tap the first row titled 好用，请开发者喝杯☕吧. Select this support option and tap the purchase button. No option is selected by default. All bookkeeping features remain available without purchasing, including after cancellation or a failed purchase. A successful tip does not create a bookkeeping transaction.

The app displays the localized price supplied by StoreKit before the user chooses a support option. Selecting a tier never purchases automatically; the explicit purchase button opens the native Apple confirmation inside the app. No currency is converted or replaced with a guessed amount. No payment information is collected by the developer. The app has no developer-operated payment server. The first three consumable support products are intended to be reviewed together with the first app version.

The review screenshot must show the final native support chooser with visible localized prices. The same three options are available; select the product being reviewed. No price is drawn into or overlaid on the screenshot.
