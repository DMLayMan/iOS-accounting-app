Environment: physical iPhone 12, iOS 26.6.1 (23G83); TestFlight 1.0 (13), bundle com.dmlayman.yuji; Xcode 27.0 (27A266a), StoreKit 2. Reproduced on 2026-10-16. No local .storekit configuration in the distributed app.

Current reproduction:
1. The user confirms their Media & Purchases country is mainland China. No account sign-out, country change or account switching was performed.
2. Open the consumable support chooser. Request Product.products(for:) and display Product.displayPrice without conversion. Refreshing returns US$0.99 / US$2.99 / US$3.99.
3. Select com.yuji.app.support.encourage. The product row and purchase button both show US$0.99.
4. The user taps the purchase button. Apple system confirmation shows CNY6, then the user cancels. The system-sheet amount is user-reported; we have not captured that sheet or independently recorded its environment badge.
5. Read back the app's verified transaction journal: no new transaction; existing receipts and financial data are unchanged. Reopening the chooser still shows USD and permits selection. No additional purchase is needed to demonstrate the mismatch.

Historical evidence (separate from build 13): on the same iPhone, TestFlight build 7 returned Storefront USA and a US$2.99 coffee quote. Two user-initiated Sandbox purchases produced StoreKit-verified CNY18 transactions. Those are historical receipts, not build 13 transactions. Raw transaction IDs, account identifiers and tokens are omitted.

Implementation checks already present in build 13:
- Listen to Storefront.updates and invalidate the cached Product list.
- Request Product.products(for:) on refresh; compare Storefront IDs before and after loading.
- Compare Product currency with Storefront currency where available.
- Refresh/check quote and storefront before calling purchase; use the onStorefrontChange purchase option.
These checks do not resolve the discrepancy between product metadata and the Apple confirmation sheet. We do not hard-code CNY, replace currency symbols, or perform currency conversion.

Expected: product metadata, displayed quote and system purchase confirmation agree for the active purchase identity, or an observable storefront change permits refreshing before confirmation.

Questions:
1. Can TestFlight catalog and purchase execution use different cached account/storefront contexts on this OS?
2. Is there a supported way to refresh this state without signing out the user's everyday account?
3. What focused diagnostics would distinguish a StoreKit service issue from account-session propagation?
4. Is App Store production affected? We have not established that production is unaffected.
